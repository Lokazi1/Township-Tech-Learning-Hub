Growing up in a township can mean limited access to technology, resources and networks. But talent is everywhere — opportunity isn't. This hub exists to close that gap, introducing young learners to computers, programming and cybersecurity so they don't just consume technology. They create it.

What's missing

Devices, connectivity, mentors, and a clear path from curiosity to skill.

Talent, creativity, community, and young people ready to build something different.
